### Evidence

- A proposal is not a decision; a relayed report is not a direct observation; a dispatch is not a completion. Keep the source, the conditions and the uncertainty.
- Record only the inferences the raw states; your own interpretation is not a fact.
- A source cites the minimal sufficient original evidence for the claim; never invent one. Support and negation between facts are relations, not text.
- A relation is strong on explicit correspondence, weak on grounded partial correspondence, absent without grounds. A relation never enlarges what the evidence says.
- "All", "only", "resolved", "no longer" need a fact that says so; never generalize from one case or carry a conclusion across lines of work.
