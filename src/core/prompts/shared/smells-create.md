**Bad smells when creating knowledge**

The commonest ways the existing principles are broken in real records, ranked by frequency and harm, to be checked item by item. The list adds no principle; judge by the principles, and handle anything not listed by them.

1. **Running log**: the content is only who did what in which ticket or commit, test counts, deployment steps, run costs, timelines. Write only the rulings, mechanisms, reasons, lessons and one entry point that still hold; with nothing to write, create nothing and leave the process at the fact layer. (Keep no running log, Useful afterwards)
2. **Duplicates existing knowledge**: an equivalent item exists, or the understanding belongs to one that must be maintained together with it, yet a new item is created. Create nothing without new understanding; where the original understanding changed, update the original item. (No new understanding → do not create, Divide by independent maintenance, Update)
3. **Claim and grounds do not match**: a conclusion, user quotation or number in the body is missing from the cited facts, or matched to the wrong object, condition, experimental group or result. Cite the facts that carry the claim. Paraphrase where the facts support the meaning but not the quoted wording; delete what has no grounds. (Citation, Distinguish evidence)
4. **The agent's words given as a user ruling**: what an agent decided, derived, proposed or read, or the part the user never answered, is written as the user's ruling. Say it comes from the agent and keep the strength of evidence; delete what has no grounds. (Distinguish evidence, Judge claim by claim)
5. **Measure lost**: a small sample or one person's judgment is stated as a measurement, or a scope, condition or "unverified" is dropped. Restore the measure and the qualifiers from the facts. (Preserve qualifiers)
6. **Identifiers or shorthand only**: a ticket or task number as the subject, or telegraphic shorthand that cannot be read outside the original discussion. Write a rule and reason that stand without the number. (Understandable outside the original discussion)
7. **Unreliable pointers**: the body depends on a temporary directory or an archived item that cannot be reliably read back. Replace it with a reliable entry point, such as a repository document or a fact id, and keep the essentials needed to understand in the body. (Only loses detail → leave it in place, Insufficient length)
8. **Several topics in one item**: several topics that need separate maintenance are written into one item. Create them as separate items by independent maintenance. (Divide by independent maintenance)
9. **Wrong scope or category**: single-project or one-off content in the global scope; progress written into `open`. Choose the scope and category by use; create nothing for pure progress. (Choose the category by use)
