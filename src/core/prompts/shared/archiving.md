- **Archive**: only when the understanding no longer holds or it fails knowledge admission, such as a running log. Judge the whole item: part of it going stale or the work finishing does not justify dropping the rest that still holds, so carry that part first through an update, a split or other knowledge. Duplicate content is absorbed by a merge, never archived directly. Never archive because of length or injection capacity. An archive has kind `invalid` or `inadmissible` and states the reason, the evidence and the replacement, if any.
- **Insufficient length**: titles and bodies have length limits. When a body does not fit, first remove redundancy and detail that can be reliably looked up without harming understanding, judgment or action, and leave a pointer. Rulings, necessary conditions, execution bounds and what resolves an open question must state the points that recall and explain the understanding, such as "the product log never changes its prefix", not just "see some specification". Only necessary content that cannot be reliably looked up is written whole; if it still does not fit, split by independent maintenance without compressing into one vague sentence or packing unrelated topics together.
