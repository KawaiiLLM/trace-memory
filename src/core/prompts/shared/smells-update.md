**Bad smells when updating knowledge**

The commonest ways the existing principles are broken in real records, ranked by frequency and harm, to be checked item by item. The list adds no principle; judge by the principles, and handle anything not listed by them.

1. **Running log**: who did what in which ticket or commit, test counts, deployment steps, run costs, timelines. Rewrite as the rulings, mechanisms, reasons, lessons and one entry point that still hold; with nothing left to keep, leave it to archive. (Keep no running log, Useful afterwards)
2. **Appended history**: "also:", "later:", "then tightened", dates in sequence. Rewrite from the current view as conclusion and reasons; keep only the times and conditions that affect meaning or judgment. (Do not stack history, Preserve qualifiers)
3. **`open` used as a ledger**: the work is finished or superseded, or the question was settled elsewhere, yet the item stays `open`. Rewrite a settled one as its conclusion under the same identity and recategorize it. Keep a still-open one with its question or risk, current grounds and entry point, and the essentials of any clear resolution condition. (Choose the category by use, State what is unresolved)
4. **Partly out of date**: something implemented still reads "not yet implemented"; part of the content has expired or been replaced by a new ruling while the rest holds. Check conditions, versions and grounds, then rewrite, naming the successor where there is one; an item that no longer holds as a whole is left to archive. (Update)
5. **Claim and grounds do not match**: a conclusion, user quotation or number in the body is missing from the cited facts, or matched to the wrong object, condition, experimental group or result. A common cause is citing only the facts nearest the latest edit. Cite the facts that carry the claim and correct the mismatch, paraphrasing where the facts support the meaning but not the quoted wording and deleting what has no grounds. (Citation, Distinguish evidence)
6. **The agent's words given as a user ruling**: what an agent decided, derived, proposed or read, or the part the user never answered, is mixed into the statement of a user ruling. Correct the attribution and keep the strength of evidence; delete what has no grounds. (Distinguish evidence, Judge claim by claim)
7. **Measure lost**: a small sample or one person's judgment is stated as a measurement, or a scope, condition or "unverified" is dropped. Restore the measure and the qualifiers from the facts. (Preserve qualifiers)
8. **Identifiers or shorthand only**: a ticket or task number as the subject, or telegraphic shorthand that cannot be read outside the original discussion. Rewrite as a rule and reason that stand without the number. (Understandable outside the original discussion)
9. **Unreliable pointers**: the body depends on a temporary directory or an archived item that cannot be reliably read back. Replace it with a reliable entry point, such as a repository document or a fact id, and keep the essentials needed to understand in the body. (Only loses detail → leave it in place, Insufficient length)
10. **Wrong scope or category**: single-project or one-off content in the global scope; a legacy category. Update the scope or category; correct only a category that is really wrong, never to make a merge easier. (Choose the category by use)
