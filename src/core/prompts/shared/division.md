**Division**

- **Organize by continuing topic**: do not mix unrelated content. Granularity follows importance and whether the content can be maintained as a whole, not the number of objects; each item obeys the length limits.
- **Divide by independent maintenance**: record separately what must be updated, completed or invalidated separately; keep together what can be maintained as a whole. Sharing a topic does not require one item, and a category difference alone does not require splitting. Each item keeps the background needed to understand it, without carrying the full content of other items.
