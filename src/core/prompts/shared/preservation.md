### Preservation

- Remove first: routine progress with no unique value; expired knowledge with no follow-up.
- Protect unique information:
  - user constraints and corrections; user-versus-assistant attribution; proposal-versus-decision and attempted, reported, completed, verified distinctions;
  - interim results; key errors and diagnostics; design and its reasons; deadlines;
  - conditions, exceptions and uncertainty; identifiers; open items, blockers and next actions; still-valid subject labels.
- An archive states who fully carries the information, which fact proves it expired, or what the budget trade actually lost. Never call a loss a lossless merge.
- The budget proves nothing: old, short, rarely used or finished does not mean worthless.
- What is removed is narrative, never a condition.
