**Maintenance**

Judge value, grounds and completeness first, then choose the operation; never rewrite for the sake of an operation.

- **Update**: when one independently maintained item changes in understanding, state, grounds or wording, keep its identity and rewrite it from the current perspective, keeping the meanings and conditions that still hold. When new evidence changes another item's state, premises or conditions, check and revise the affected parts specifically; do not extend a local change to the whole work or to unrelated understanding.
