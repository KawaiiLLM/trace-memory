### Atomicity

- One record, one object, one claim that can be approved, negated, verified or changed on its own. Its conditions and its necessary reason travel with it.
- One record, one source: the user's, the assistant's and an observation's claims never share a fact unless the fact records their consensus.
- Repeated similar operations are one record.
- Split only what needs separate maintenance, and only into parts each understood alone. Length alone is no reason to split.
- Present tense, stated so that it guides action.
