### Topic organization

- Organize by a continuing topic; do not mix unrelated content. Granularity follows importance, whether the content can be maintained together, and budget, not the number of objects.
- Decide Admission first, then choose the closest category for the main use. Different categories alone do not require splitting a topic.
- Belonging to one piece of work does not make content one item. Organize parts separately when they need independent updates, completion or invalidation. Do not tie a lasting ruling's retention to implementation progress; keep the ruling together with its necessary reasons.

**Example: a ruling maintained apart from progress.** Illustrative only, not evidence or an admission rule.

> Mixed item: The user requires delivery state to be maintained uniformly by the database; the implementation is deployed; the slow recovery is not yet investigated.
> Handling: Maintain the lasting ruling and its necessary reasons on their own. Record the slow recovery, still worth tracking, as a separate open matter. Leave the deployment process in facts unless it still affects the next judgment.
> A completed implementation is not evidence that the open matter is resolved.

This shows independent maintenance only; it does not prescribe how many knowledge items a piece of work must produce.
