### Atomicity

- One item records one claim or finding — one object, one content — that changes on its own. When the claim or finding is revised, the body is replaced, not appended with history.
- When the claim's content changes, update the original item, so that the change and its historical evidence can be traced; different independently changing claims about one object are maintained apart.
- `scope` defaults to `project`. Only a user preference or a general working method that stays valid across projects is `global`; knowledge valid only in this session is `session`.
