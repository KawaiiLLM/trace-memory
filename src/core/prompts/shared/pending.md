### Pending matters

- First decide whether a claim is worth keeping resident, then whether the conclusion holds or something remains unresolved; `open` and `dispute` are not containers for ungrounded content.
- A conclusion with reliable evidence that it holds enters its category. A core claim needs the user's explicit recognition or direct observation within the scope it claims; relation chains help trace the evidence and never raise a source's authority or degree of proof. The user's approval of a plan does not prove the plan's technical effect.
- `open`: worth tracking, yet still awaiting an answer, adoption, verification or completion; state what is unresolved, who proposed it, and what decision or evidence it waits for.
- `dispute`: incompatible claims about one object under the same conditions, with evidence insufficient to explain or decide; state both sides and their grounds. Evidence that the conflict exists is required; proof that one side holds is not.
- Differences caused by version or condition are not disputes by themselves; an assistant's alternative never manufactures a dispute to weaken a user ruling still in force.
- A pending claim never becomes a confirmed conclusion by merging; duplicate pending items may merge and stay pending. Once resolved, update the original identity first; merge only where a duplicate identity truly exists.
