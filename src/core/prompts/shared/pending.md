### Pending matters

- `open` and `dispute` knowledge are unresolved matters still in doubt.
- Reliability of fact evidence: by source, user > observation > assistant; by category, decision > interpretation > proposal > question. A decision or interpretation stated by the user, or an objective observation, is reliable evidence.
- A claim without reliable evidence belongs to `open`. Typically the core claim of the facts it rests on comes from the assistant or is a proposal or question, and no reliable evidence explicitly supports it.
- A claim whose reliable evidence conflicts belongs to `dispute`. Typically the core claim of the facts it rests on is supported and negated at once by several pieces of reliable evidence.
- Pending matters still follow Admission, Completeness and the other principles; when existing knowledge becomes pending, keep the background of the doubt intelligible.
