# 20b — Hard material budgets, token-based Consolidation batches, capacity re-freeze

**Parent:** 20 — Shared memory material and bounded compaction (sections "Shared material budgets", "Token admission and batch progress", the "Capacity negotiation" bullet, and the Configuration bullet; acceptance scenarios 3–8, 15, 17).

**What to build:** the ruled default limits become hard, measured on the exact rendered view with the existing estimator: knowledge block 10,000; historical facts plus current task material 20,000 shared; compressed Raw at most 10,000 inside that block; Noting trigger 10,000 and batch at most 10,000 normal-view tokens (oldest contiguous whole-entry prefix, no skipping); Consolidation trigger 5,000 and batch at most 10,000 rendered fact tokens (oldest-first whole-fact prefix, same representation for trigger and selection), replacing the fifty-fact trigger; fresh material at most 30,000 in total. Boundary accounting as ruled: entry/fact views with their own labels, markers and joining separators count against the 10,000 current-material ceiling; outer headings, the range and block receipts against the enclosing 20,000; knowledge framing and its receipt against the knowledge budget; every emitted component counted exactly once. The knowledge cap keeps category priority and deterministic order but loses the exemption for constraints, open items and disputes; retained items are whole, omitted items stay stored and traceable, receipts are bounded. Remaining episodic space is filled with historical facts in the existing freshness order after the selected current material and mandatory cues are reserved. When a host's reported capacity is below the domain ceiling, core reduces the oldest-first task set and re-freezes write eligibility and audit membership together before execution; an indivisible oldest unit that cannot fit stays pending with a capacity error, never clipped, skipped or marked processed. Configuration: `noting.batchTokens` default 10,000; new `consolidation.triggerTokens` and `consolidation.batchTokens`; `consolidation.triggerUnconsolidatedFacts` removed with an explicit removed-key error; one effective Raw ceiling shared by Noting and compact.

**User confirmations (2026-09-08):** the knowledge hard cap without the constraint exemption is accepted, on the understanding that constraints keep first priority and omitted items remain traceable.
